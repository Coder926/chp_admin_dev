# Bookings Help

## Overview

The Bookings screen lets you view, search, and manage customer bookings across the platform.

## Features

- **List view** — Browse all bookings with pagination
- **Detail panel** — Click a booking to see full details in a slide-over
- **Filters** — Narrow down bookings by status, region, or date range

## Managing Bookings

- Click a booking row to open its detail panel
- Status changes and updates are reflected immediately
